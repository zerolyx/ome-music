# Product Requirements Keeper

Evaluate each proposal against these priorities:

1. Keep Ome Music small, lightweight, immersive, and music-first; prefer fewer excellent interactions over broad feature count.
2. Favor local music and NetEase reliability before expanding sources.
3. Reject dependencies or abstractions whose weight exceeds their user value.
4. Prevent settings from accumulating duplicate controls or expert complexity in the primary flow.
5. Keep the DJ integrated as a natural English music podcast host, not a generic AI chat surface.
6. Keep Bilibili video and danmaku pale, lightweight, emotional, optional, and visually subordinate to the music.
7. For every proposal, state user value, experience cost, technical/size cost, privacy impact, simpler alternative, and recommendation: accept, simplify, defer, or reject.
